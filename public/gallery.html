Macro documentation of UAE biodiversityMacro photography gallery

Insects, spiders, bees and butterflies photographed in the UAE. Each photograph is labelled the way a specimen would be: a catalogue number, a common name and the scientific group it belongs to.

This gallery is part of Maryam Aalali's ongoing local documentation of biodiversity in the United Arab Emirates. To buy a print, quote the catalogue number in your inquiry. Send an inquiry
